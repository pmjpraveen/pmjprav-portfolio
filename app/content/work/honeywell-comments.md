## Hard to find, hard to read | The challenge

Comments were hard to find and hard to understand, so new users struggled to get started. The app also didn't let people personalise their view or use widgets.

![Three problems: comments hard to find, hard to understand, and a fixed view](/projects/honeywell-comments/illus-problems.png "Illustration · Three problems kept new users from getting started.")

::: rows
Finding | Comments were hard to locate in the dashboard.
Understanding | Once found, they were hard to make sense of.
Personalising | There was no way to personalise the view or use widgets.
:::

## What changed | Results

::: rows
Problem | Comments were hard to find and understand, and new users struggled.
Decision | Redesign comments into a simpler, more organised layout.
Outcome | Comments are easier to find and use.
:::

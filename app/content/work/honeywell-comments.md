## Comments were hard to find and hard to understand | 01 · Problem

![Three problems: comments hard to find, hard to understand, and a fixed view](/projects/honeywell-comments/illus-problems.png "Illustration · Three problems kept new users from getting started.")

1. **Hard to find.** Comments were hard to locate in the dashboard.
2. **Hard to understand.** Once found, they were hard to make sense of.
3. **A fixed view.** There was no way to personalise the view or use widgets.

Together, they meant ==new users struggled to get started==.

## A simpler, more organised layout | 02 · Results

::: rows

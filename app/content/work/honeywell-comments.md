## Challenge

The feature was hard to use because it had too many confusing parts. People struggled to find things and to understand how comments worked, which made it harder for new users to get started. The app also didn't let people personalise things or use widgets.

## Results

The new feature has a simple, organised layout that makes it easier for users to find what they need and move around.

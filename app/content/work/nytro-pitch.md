## The app was hard to find your way around, and slow to create in | 01 · Problem

![Four problems: hard to find, slow to create, no customisation, no reports](/projects/nytro-pitch/illus-problems.png "Illustration · Four problems, each one making the product harder to learn.")

1. **Hard to find.** The layout was confusing, so people struggled to find important features.
2. **Slow to create.** Authors had trouble creating content, which made the product harder for new users to learn.
3. **No customisation.** Users couldn't customise anything.
4. **No reports.** There were no reports.

## Design by hypothesis: form a view, test it, refine it | 02 · Process

![Five steps: analysis, information architecture, wireframes and prototype, usability testing, visual design and style guide](/projects/nytro-pitch/illus-process.png "Illustration · Each test fed back into the design.")

1. **Analysis.** The Customer Success team gathered customer feedback on the challenges users faced.
2. **Information architecture.** Using that feedback, I redesigned the layout and content ==to put user needs first==.
3. **Wireframes and prototype.** I sketched the new design, refined it from user feedback, then built a detailed prototype to test.
4. **Usability testing.** I tested with my team, the Customer Success team and a range of users, and improved the design from their feedback.
5. **Style guide.** I defined a consistent look, covering colour, type and icons, and wrote a guide to keep it consistent in later updates.

## Three core areas, rebuilt | 03 · Redesign

::: rows
Problem | Inconsistent actions, unstructured cards and reports that didn't explain themselves.
Decision | Redesign the three areas that changed most: the pitch overview, categories and reports.
Outcome | ==Features are easier to find==, and the content creation flow is shorter.
:::

Each pair shows the original on the left and the redesign on the right.

### Pitch overview
**Problem.** Actions were inconsistent, cards had no structure, and the menu disappeared over thumbnails.

![Pitch overview, before](/projects/nytro-pitch/pitch-overview-before.png "Before")
![Pitch overview, after](/projects/nytro-pitch/pitch-overview-after.png "After")

- Actions and buttons were inconsistent, such as the plus button and the button beside the search field.
- Cards had no structured presentation of information, and pitch thumbnails varied from card to card.
- The kebab menu was hard to see when a thumbnail image was present.

::: decision What a pitch card shows
Options | Both "Created date" and "Published date" on every card · Each date only where it's needed
Chose | Each date only where it's needed
Because | Showing both dates needed a reason, so each stays only where it's needed.
:::

### Pitch categories
**Problem.** The navigation lacked contrast, and the category list said nothing about its pitches.

![Pitch categories, before](/projects/nytro-pitch/pitch-categories-before.png "Before")
![Pitch categories, after](/projects/nytro-pitch/pitch-categories-after.png "After")

- The selected navigation item, "Categories", lacked contrast against the others.
- The category listing was not visually appealing.
- Each category could show how many pitches are published, drafted and so on.

### Pitch reports
**Problem.** Reports were cluttered, and their numbers didn't say what they measured.

![Pitch reports, before](/projects/nytro-pitch/pitch-reports-before.png "Before")
![Pitch reports, after](/projects/nytro-pitch/pitch-reports-after.png "After")

- The individual pitch report was cluttered, with inconsistent actions and buttons, and KPIs and graphs that departed from the guidelines used elsewhere in the product.
- On the overall pitch report, KPI names did not say what each KPI measured.
- On an individual submission, the section cards were not informative. The page looked empty, then a sudden "53%" appeared in the corner, so content felt detached.

## 84% engagement, and CSAT up 26% | 04 · Impact

![84% engagement, CSAT up 26% from a 34% baseline, and three changes behind them](/projects/nytro-pitch/illus-results.png "Illustration · Easier to find, faster to make, more to engage with.")

1. **84% customer engagement,** and ==CSAT up 26% from a 34% baseline==.
2. **Features are easier to find,** because the layout puts user needs first.
3. **Content is quicker to create,** with a shorter flow, so pitches take less time to make.
4. **More to engage with,** from the new reports and customisation.

> “With our fresh visual branding and refined language, the pitch app distinctly encapsulates the essence of both our current and target customer base, as well as our core values.”
> — Ravish Kamath, Founder and CEO, Nytro.ai

## Start from what customers say | 05 · Reflection

1. **Start from what customers say.** The redesign began with the feedback Customer Success gathered from customers.
2. **Test with more than one group.** My team, Customer Success and a range of users all tested it, and each round improved the design.
3. **Write the system down.** A style guide ==kept later updates consistent== after the redesign shipped.
